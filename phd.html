A PLAN THAT STARTS WITH YOU

PhD & research,
with a clearer direction.

A strong research application begins with a clear question and the right academic environment. We help you organise your background, research interests and the next steps in your university search.

  • Clarify your research field and academic objectives
  • Research relevant departments and supervisor interests
  • Organise your research proposal and academic profile
  • Explore advertised funding and individual entry criteria
Discuss this pathway

Start your conversation with

  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Research proposal or statement of research interests
  • Academic CV, publications or relevant project work
  • References and programme-specific language evidence

The final document list depends on the institution and programme. Our team will help you review the relevant requirements.
