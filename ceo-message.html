25+ YEARS OF LEADERSHIPOur story

Welcome to Genesis Educare.

When I founded Genesis Educare in 2025, it was built on a single, uncompromising principle: unwavering trust and student-first guidance.

My journey into international educational consultancy is rooted in over 25 years of corporate management and banking experience. I began my career in 1998 as a Credit Officer at Prime Bank Limited in Bangladesh. Over the years, I had the privilege of leading various key branches as a Branch Manager for 17 years. In recognition of my dedication and performance, I was honoured with the Best Manager Award in 2009. The following year, in 2010, I was appointed as the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Prime Bank’s overseas subsidiary, Prime Exchange Co. Pte Ltd, in Singapore. Later, in 2017, I joined Jamuna Bank Limited, managing two of its most prominent financial hubs — Motijheel and Shantinagar branches. In 2022, I decided to step down from formal banking to pursue my passion for entrepreneurship.

Throughout my quarter-century in top-tier banking, I learned that true success relies entirely on exceptional customer service, transparency, and deep customer trust.

Why Genesis Educare?

My transition into the education consulting industry stems from a personal experience. In 2022, while looking for higher education opportunities for my own daughter in Canada, I consulted several student consultancy agencies in Dhaka. What I observed was concerning: a prevalent gap in trust and sincerity. Many agencies prioritised high commission rates over a student’s true potential, often directing bright students away from top-tier institutions simply to maximise their own financial gains.

As a parent, I recognised the urgent need for an agency that operates with high ethical standards — one that treats every student’s career as if it were their own child’s future. I resolved to build a platform that never misguides students for higher commissions, but instead provides genuine, profile-centric career guidance.

Our global reach

Genesis Educare began its venture by guiding students to China. Today, through our commitment and expanding network, we successfully place students across the globe — including Malaysia, South Korea, Japan, China, USA, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Germany, France, Greece, Hungary, Malta, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Bulgaria, and many other destinations.

At Genesis Educare, your dreams are guided by experienced hands, transparent counsel, and genuine care. We look forward to being a trusted companion on your journey to global education.
